# Refunds & Returns

Returns are governed by the **merchant's** return policy; SplitWave processes the refund
after the merchant approves it.

Once a merchant issues a refund to SplitWave:

- The refund is applied to the shopper's **remaining unpaid installments first**.
- Any amount beyond the unpaid balance is returned to the shopper's payment method
  within **3–10 business days**.
- Partial refunds reduce the final installments first (last installment shrinks first).

If a merchant has approved a return but the refund hasn't appeared after 10 business
days, escalate to a human agent with the order ID. SplitWave cannot force a merchant to
accept a return.
